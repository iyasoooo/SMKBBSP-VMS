4.2 Interface design — wireframe

Wireframes for the SMKBBSP VisitorPass system. The visitor and teacher pages are shown in mobile view, the security guard pages in tablet view, and the administrator pages in laptop view, because that is the device each user actually works on.

4.2.1 Visitor (Mobile View)

The visitor has no account. These pages are opened on a phone.

4.2.2 Teacher (Mobile View)

The teacher approves or rejects requests, usually on a phone between classes.

4.2.3 Security Guard (Tablet View)

The guard works at the gate on a tablet, so the buttons are large and the lists are easy to read.

4.2.4 Administrator (Laptop View)

The administrator uses a laptop in the office, so these pages use a side menu and wider tables.
